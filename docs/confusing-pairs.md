# Confusing pairs

| Word A | Word B | Distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| alltid | bestandig | Both mean *always*; register varies |
| alltid | for alltid | Always vs. forever |
| bedre | begge | Better vs. both |
| sier | ser | Says vs. sees |
| igjen | ingen | Again vs. nobody / none |
| vanlig | vanskelig | Common / usual vs. difficult |
| veldig | vanlig | Very vs. common / usual |
| forelska | forveksler | In love vs. confuses / mixes up |
| mot | imot | Toward / against / courage vs. against |
| vite | kjenne | Know a fact vs. know / be familiar with |
| gå | dra | Go / walk vs. travel / leave |

**Jeg ser deg.** — I see you.

**Hva sier du?** — What are you saying?

**Forelskelse er vanlig blant ungdommer.** — Falling in love is common
among young people.
