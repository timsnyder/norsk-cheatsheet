# Everyday phrases

| Norwegian | English | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hvordan har du det? | How are you? | General check-in |
| Går det bra med deg? | Are you OK? | Natural, often concerned |
| Er alt bra med deg? | Is everything OK with you? | Checking on someone |
| Har du det bra? | Are you doing well? | Well-being |
| Jeg hører på deg. | I'm listening to you. | Attentive listening |
| Jeg savner deg. | I miss you. | Personal |
| Jeg prøver å lære norsk. | I'm trying to learn Norwegian. | Learning |
| Jeg kommer til å savne deg. | I'm going to miss you. | Future |
| Jeg vil alltid savne deg. | I will always miss you. | `alltid` |
| Jeg kommer til å savne deg for alltid. | I'll miss you forever. | `for alltid` |

**Går det bra med deg?** is often more concerned than the general greeting
**Hvordan har du det?** Context and tone matter.
