# Verbs

## Common conjugations

| Infinitive | Present | Past | Past participle | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| å være | er | var | vært | to be |
| å ha | har | hadde | hatt | to have |
| å prøve | prøver | prøvde | prøvd | to try |
| å savne | savner | savnet | savnet | to miss |
| å skulle | skal | skulle | skullet | shall / will / be supposed to |
| å kunne | kan | kunne | kunnet | can / be able to |
| å ville | vil | ville | villet | want / will |
| å måtte | må | måtte | måttet | must / have to |
| å burde | bør | burde | burdet | should |

**Jeg prøver å lære norsk.** — I am trying to learn Norwegian.

**Jeg skal prøve igjen.** — I will try again.

**Prøv igjen!** — Try again!

## Modal verbs

Modal verbs normally take a **bare infinitive** (no `å`).

- **Jeg skal lære norsk.** — I will learn Norwegian.
- **Jeg kan snakke norsk.** — I can speak Norwegian.
- **Jeg vil se deg.** — I want to see you.
- **Jeg må dra.** — I have to leave.

Compare **Jeg prøver å lære norsk**, where **å prøve** is not a modal verb.

## Future expressions: skal, kommer til å, vil

English *will* does not map to just one Norwegian expression.

| Expression | Common emphasis | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `skal` | Plan, intention, commitment, or expectation | Jeg skal ringe deg. |
| `kommer til å` | Expected future outcome | Det kommer til å regne. |
| `vil` | Desire/willingness or prediction, depending on context | Jeg vil hjelpe deg. |

- **Jeg skal ringe deg.** — I'll call you (a plan or commitment).
- **Det kommer til å regne.** — It's going to rain (a prediction).
- **Jeg vil hjelpe deg.** — I want to help you / I'm willing to help you.
- **Det vil ta tid.** — It will take time (a prediction).

**Jeg vil alltid savne deg** is grammatical and may express an enduring
prediction or sentiment. However, **vil** can also evoke willingness or
intention, so **Jeg kommer alltid til å savne deg** is a clearer choice
when you simply predict that you will always miss someone.

**Jeg skal savne deg** is possible in context but can sound less natural
than **Jeg kommer til å savne deg** for an involuntary feeling.

These are tendencies, not hard rules: `skal` and `vil` can also express
predictions, and context determines the intended meaning.
