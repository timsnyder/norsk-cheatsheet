# Pronunciation

Norwegian pronunciation varies by dialect. These are learning notes, not
a universal phonetic transcription.

- **å** often resembles the vowel in English *law* (dialect-dependent).
- **ø** has no exact general American English equivalent; round the lips
  while producing a front vowel.
- **æ** often resembles the vowel in English *cat*.
- **kj** and **skj** traditionally represent different sounds, although
  some speakers merge them.
- Written Bokmål does not prescribe one spoken accent.

For reliable audio, listen to native-speaker examples and note the dialect.
